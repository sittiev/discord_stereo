Info and troubleshooting
Can't find my client
The scan covers stable, PTB, canary and Vencord in each OS's standard paths. Flatpak and Snap are not supported in this version.
Validation failed, nothing was written
The binary changed version and the offset table no longer matches. The wizard never writes to an unknown binary.
No stereo on my headphones
Local loopback is mono by design. The receiver needs a stereo decoder, and the content needs real per-channel differences.
